Frequently Asked Questions

What is the "No-Fix, No-Fee" policy?

If we diagnose a software issue and are unable to resolve or offer a clear repair path, you won't be charged for the repair attempt.

How do drop-offs and on-site visits work?

You can arrange a convenient drop-off time in Cheltenham free of charge, or request an on-site visit anywhere in Gloucestershire for a small £15 call-out fee.

Are hardware replacement parts included in these prices?

Labor rates cover diagnosis and technical work. Replacement parts (e.g. new SSD, RAM stick, motherboard) are charged separately at cost price with your approval.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, bank transfer (BACS), and major debit/credit cards upon service completion.
