Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who I am

Coast Turtle's IT Land is run by Antonio Sandu, providing IT support in Cheltenham and Gloucestershire. I am responsible for the personal information described here. You can contact me at antoniosandu21@gmail.com or on 07391580090.

What I collect

  • Quote and contact requests: your name, phone number, email address, the services you're interested in, any estimate, and your message.
  • Appointment requests: the same contact details, plus the service, preferred date, time and location, and any notes.
  • Reviews: your name, rating, review text, and any photos you choose to attach.
  • Page visits: the address of the page viewed and the time. No cookies are used and this is not linked to you.
  • Readability setting: if you turn on the dyslexia-friendly font, that choice is saved in your own browser only.

Why I use it

I use your details to reply to your enquiry, arrange and carry out the work you ask for, and keep records of that work. This is necessary to take steps at your request before providing a service, and to provide it. Reviews are published only with your permission, which you give when you submit one. Your information is never sold or used for marketing.

Who processes it for me

  • Render hosts this website and the database where requests are stored.
  • Google (Gmail) receives the email notifications of new requests.
  • FormSubmit and Web3Forms are email-delivery services used to forward form submissions and reviews to my inbox.
  • WhatsApp, only if you choose to contact me that way.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK; they are expected to protect it to UK GDPR standards.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to any work are deleted within 12 months. Records of work carried out are kept for up to 6 years for tax and accounting purposes. Unpublished reviews are deleted once reviewed; you can ask for a published review to be removed at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it is used, by emailing antoniosandu21@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
